# Alliance Holdings

> Alliance Holdings GP, L.P. (NASDAQ: AHGP) was a limited partnership that owned and controlled
> Alliance Resource Management GP, LLC, the managing general partner of Alliance Resource Partners,
> L.P. (NASDAQ: ARLP). In 2018 AHGP was absorbed into ARLP in a simplification transaction and
> ceased to exist as a separate public entity; AHGP unitholders received ARLP common units. Alliance
> Resource Partners continues as a diversified energy company earning income from coal production
> and from coal, oil and gas mineral royalties across the United States.
